# Halden Systems renewal policy

Halden Systems makes a work-management platform: teams plan projects, assign tasks, and track work in it. Halden licenses seats on three tiers: Basic, Standard, and Premium. All renewals are for one year and must include at least as many seats as the customer licenses today. More seats are allowed, whether the tier stays the same or changes.

## What each plan includes

Each plan includes everything in the plan below it.

- **Basic:** projects, tasks, and boards; file sharing and comments; the mobile app.
- **Standard:** workflow automation (rules that route work, update statuses, and send reminders); integrations with email, calendar, CRM, and chat tools; shared dashboards.
- **Premium:** single sign-on and automatic user provisioning; a full audit log of changes; approval steps with recorded sign-off; admin roles and data-retention controls.

## Annual price

The annual per-unit rate is the current annual subscription amount divided by the number of currently licensed seats. For a renewal on the current tier, the price base is that rate multiplied by the number of seats in the quote. Choose an annual price from that base up to the ceiling for the account's utilization band. The ceiling is a limit, not a required increase.

Utilization is usage divided by currently licensed units (seats), not by the number in the new quote. Apply these thresholds in descending order and use the first one the account meets. Each lower bound is inclusive.

| Minimum utilization (`min_utilization`) | Utilization range | Maximum increase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.00 | 100% or more | 15% |
| 0.80 | At least 80%, below 100% | 10% |
| 0.00 | Below 80% | 4% |

The ceiling is the quote's price base plus the permitted percentage increase. These bands apply across all tiers, including allowed tier changes. Do not add an increase above the ceiling to cover another charge.

## Timing and plan changes

Send renewal quotes no earlier than 45 days before the contract end date. The first day of that window is included. A quote prepared in advance must still wait until the window opens before it is sent.

A one-tier upgrade is allowed: Basic to Standard, or Standard to Premium. Its price base is 1.50 × the annual per-unit rate × the number of seats in the quote. Apply the same utilization band to this base. Premium has no higher tier, and a two-tier upgrade is not allowed.

A customer may request a move down one tier at renewal: Premium to Standard, or Standard to Basic. There is no tier below Basic. Do not initiate a downgrade or treat a request for a lower price on the existing plan as a request to change tiers. All renewals, including requested downgrades, have a one-year term.

No discounts, account credit, free months, or multi-year terms are allowed. These restrictions apply to promises in correspondence as well as to the quote itself.

## Disputed invoices

Only a rep can resolve a billing dispute. Escalate the dispute for the rep to handle; the renewals agent cannot settle it independently. Do not describe a disputed invoice as resolved before the rep has resolved it, or offer a renewal concession in place of resolution.

Rep time is limited, and each escalation uses that capacity. Reserve it for issues that need a rep rather than escalating every renewal. An escalation does not authorize an exception to the commercial rules above.
